Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about planning a Tanzania safari.

How do I book?

Send an inquiry through the booking page or WhatsApp. We will discuss dates, interests and itinerary options.

Can my safari be customized?

Yes. We can tailor destinations, activities, accommodation and pacing around your priorities.

What should I bring?

Light clothing, sturdy shoes, sun protection, insect repellent, binoculars and your camera are useful starting points.

Do you offer birdwatching?

Yes. Birdwatching is a core interest of Birds and Environment Safaris and can be built into a dedicated itinerary.

Can families travel?

Yes, subject to the suitability of activities and accommodation. Tell us the ages of travelers when requesting an itinerary.

How do I contact you?

WhatsApp +255 716 108 475 or birdsandenvironment@outlook.com.
