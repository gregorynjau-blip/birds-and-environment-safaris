Ruaha Wildlife Safari

3 Days / 2 Nights   •   From US$750 per person

Starting price varies according to accommodation, season and group size.

Day 1 — Dar es Salaam / Arrival & Ruaha

Travel towards Ruaha National Park and settle into your accommodation. Depending on arrival time, enjoy an introductory game drive and begin discovering the park's remarkable landscapes and wildlife.

Day 2 — Full-Day Ruaha Wildlife Experience

Spend a full day exploring Ruaha. Look for elephants, lions, giraffes, buffalo, wild dogs and other wildlife. Enjoy a picnic lunch in the park before continuing your afternoon game drive.

Day 3 — Morning Game Drive & Departure

Enjoy an early morning wildlife drive when animals are active. After breakfast, begin your journey back towards your next destination.

Nyerere Wildlife & River Safari

3 Days / 2 Nights   •   From US$850 per person

Starting price varies according to accommodation, season and group size.

Day 1 — Arrival & Game Drive

Arrive in Nyerere National Park and settle into your accommodation. Enjoy an afternoon game drive searching for elephants, giraffes, buffalo, lions and other wildlife.

Day 2 — Wildlife & River Experience

Spend the day experiencing the park's combination of wildlife and waterways. Enjoy a boat safari where conditions permit, looking for hippos, crocodiles and birdlife along the river.

Day 3 — Morning Safari & Departure

Enjoy an early morning game drive before breakfast and departure from the park.
