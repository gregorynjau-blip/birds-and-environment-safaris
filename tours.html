Our Specialty

Southern Tanzania Safaris

Southern Tanzania is our specialty. We focus on authentic, less-crowded wildlife experiences in some of Tanzania's most remarkable southern destinations.

🐘

Ruaha National Park

Ruaha is ideal for travelers who want a true wilderness safari. Expect wide open landscapes, baobabs, elephants, lions, giraffes, wild dogs and excellent birding, with fewer vehicles in many areas than the better-known northern parks.

From US$1,200 pp

Suggested itinerary:
3 Days / 2 Nights

Day 1 – Arrive in the Ruaha area and enjoy an afternoon game drive as wildlife becomes active.
Day 2 – Spend a full day exploring different habitats, following wildlife and stopping for photography and birdwatching.
Day 3 – Enjoy a final morning game drive before departure.

🦛

Nyerere National Park

Nyerere combines classic game viewing with the special character of the Rufiji River system. Guests can experience boat safaris for hippos, crocodiles and waterbirds, as well as land-based drives for elephants, giraffes, buffalo and predators.

From US$1,100 pp

Suggested itinerary:
3 Days / 2 Nights

Day 1 – Arrive and take an afternoon/evening boat safari on the river.
Day 2 – Full-day game drive through the park, with time for wildlife viewing and photography.
Day 3 – Morning activity, then depart for your next destination.

Book This Safari

🦁

Mikumi National Park

Mikumi is a practical choice for a shorter Southern Tanzania safari and can be reached more easily from Dar es Salaam than the more remote parks. The open Mkata floodplain offers good opportunities for elephants, giraffes, zebras, buffalo, lions and a wide range of birds.

From US$650 pp

Suggested itinerary:
2 Days / 1 Night

Day 1 – Travel to Mikumi, check in and head out for an afternoon game drive.
Day 2 – Early morning game drive when wildlife is active, followed by breakfast and departure.

Book This Safari

🌿

Udzungwa Mountains

Udzungwa is for travelers who want forest, hiking and nature rather than only traditional game drives. Walk through mountain forest to waterfalls and look for forest birds, primates and plants found in this distinctive part of Southern Tanzania.

From US$650 pp

Suggested itinerary:
2 Days / 1 Night

Day 1 – Arrive and settle in before a guided nature or forest experience.
Day 2 – Take a guided mountain walk, explore the forest and waterfalls, enjoy birdwatching, then depart.

Additional Tanzania Experiences

Northern Tanzania Safaris

Southern Tanzania is our specialty. We also arrange tailor-made safaris to Northern Tanzania's famous wildlife destinations.

🦁

Serengeti National Park

Serengeti is the classic choice for travelers who want expansive grasslands and some of Africa's most famous wildlife. Game drives can bring you close to lions, cheetahs, leopards, elephants, giraffes, zebras and large herds, while the timing of the Great Migration depends on the season and location of the herds.

From US$1,400 pp

Suggested itinerary:
4 Days / 3 Nights

Day 1 – Enter the Serengeti and enjoy an afternoon game drive.
Day 2 – Full-day game viewing across the plains, following current wildlife activity.
Day 3 – Continue exploring different areas of the park, with time for photography.
Day 4 – Morning game drive and departure.

Book This Safari

🏞️

Ngorongoro Crater

Ngorongoro offers a dramatic crater landscape where wildlife gathers on the crater floor. A crater game drive can give you opportunities to see lions, elephants, buffalo, hippos, flamingos and other wildlife within a relatively compact area, while the surrounding highlands add a very different landscape.

From US$850 pp

Suggested itinerary:
2 Days / 1 Night

Day 1 – Travel to the Ngorongoro area, with time to enjoy the highland scenery.
Day 2 – Descend into the crater early for a game drive, picnic or lunch stop as appropriate, then continue onward after the crater experience.

🐘

Tarangire National Park

Tarangire is famous for its large elephants, ancient baobabs and seasonal wildlife concentrations around the Tarangire River. It is especially appealing to photographers and birdwatchers because of the varied landscapes and strong wildlife activity around water.

From US$850 pp

Suggested itinerary:
2 Days / 1 Night

Day 1 – Arrive and take an afternoon game drive through baobab country.
Day 2 – Early morning game drive, exploring river areas and other habitats before departure.

🦩

Lake Manyara National Park

Lake Manyara packs several habitats into a relatively small park: groundwater forest, open woodland, lake shore and seasonal wetlands. It is a good destination for guests interested in scenery, birdlife and wildlife such as elephants, giraffes, zebras and buffalo.

From US$850 pp

Suggested itinerary:
2 Days / 1 Night

Day 1 – Enter the park and explore the forest and open areas on an afternoon game drive.
Day 2 – Morning game drive around the lake and different habitats, then continue to your next destination.
