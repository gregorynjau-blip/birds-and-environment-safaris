About Birds and Environment Safaris

Our Story

Birds and Environment Safaris is a Tanzania-based safari company specializing in authentic Southern Tanzania wildlife and birdwatching experiences.

Our goal is to introduce travelers to the beauty of Southern Tanzania while promoting responsible tourism, environmental awareness and conservation.

Our Safari Experience

With strong knowledge of Tanzania's tourism industry and a passion for nature, we create memorable journeys through destinations such as Mikumi National Park, Ruaha National Park, Nyerere National Park and Udzungwa Mountains National Park.

Every safari is carefully designed to provide genuine wildlife encounters, cultural connections and unforgettable moments in nature.

Our Mission

To deliver responsible safari experiences that connect people with wildlife and nature while supporting conservation and protecting the environment for future generations.
