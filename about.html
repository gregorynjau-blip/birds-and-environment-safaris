About Birds and Environment Safaris

Our Story

Birds and Environment Safaris is a Tanzania-based safari company dedicated to creating authentic wildlife and birdwatching experiences while promoting respect for nature and conservation.

Our mission is to connect travelers with Tanzania's extraordinary landscapes, wildlife, cultures and birdlife through responsible and memorable journeys.

Founder & Safari Experience

Founded by a passionate Tanzanian tourism professional with years of experience guiding travelers across Tanzania, Birds and Environment Safaris combines local knowledge, hospitality and a deep love for the environment.

From wildlife encounters in national parks to discovering unique bird species, every safari is designed with care, safety and unforgettable experiences in mind.
