About Us

A safari company built around nature and people.

We design tailor-made adventures for travelers who want more than a checklist of animals. Our trips connect guests with landscapes, wildlife, birds, local communities and the people who know Tanzania best.

Southern Tanzania is at the heart of our work, with experiences across Ruaha, Nyerere, Mikumi and Udzungwa, while additional Tanzania and Zanzibar arrangements can be tailored to your interests.
