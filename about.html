About Birds and Environment Safaris

Your trusted local safari specialist for unforgettable wildlife, bird watching, and nature adventures in Southern Tanzania.

Our Story

Birds and Environment Safaris was founded with a passion for sharing the incredible wildlife, birds, and natural beauty of Southern Tanzania. We believe every safari should be personal, authentic, educational, and unforgettable.

Our goal is to connect travelers with Tanzania's wild landscapes while promoting responsible tourism that supports conservation and local communities.

Meet the Founder

Gregory Njau

Founder & Safari Guide

Gregory Njau is passionate about wildlife conservation, bird watching, and creating memorable safari experiences. With a strong interest in Southern Tanzania's national parks, he is dedicated to helping guests discover the beauty of Ruaha, Nyerere, Mikumi, Udzungwa, and other remarkable destinations.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional safari experiences that inspire a deeper appreciation for wildlife, birds, nature, and environmental conservation while delivering professional, safe, and personalized service to every guest.
