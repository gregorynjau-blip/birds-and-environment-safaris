Tanzania Wildlife Guide

Learn about the wildlife and birdlife that make Tanzania extraordinary.

Big Wildlife

Expect opportunities for elephants, giraffes, buffalo, lions, hippos and many other species depending on destination and season.

Birdwatching

Southern Tanzania offers varied habitats for both resident and migrant birds. Specialist birding itineraries can be tailored to your interests.

Wildlife Ethics

Never feed animals, chase wildlife or pressure guides to break park rules. Patient observation creates better encounters.

Photography

Use long lenses where possible, keep noise low and allow animals space to behave naturally.
